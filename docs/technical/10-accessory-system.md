# **Accessory System — Full Specification**

Accessories are equippable items that modify combat stats, dice rolls, abilities, and outcomes. They add build depth and strategic flexibility.

--------------------

## **1. Accessory Categories**

### **1.1 Offensive Accessories**
Boost attack, crit, or dice size.

Examples:

- Sharpened Fang (+1 attack)  
- Ember Charm (+10% crit chance)  
- Weighted Dice (upgrade D6 → D8)  

### **1.2 Defensive Accessories**
Boost defense, block, or resistances.

Examples:

- Bark Shield (+2 defense)  
- Water Veil (reduce fire damage)  
- Guardian Stone (chance to negate hit)  

### **1.3 Utility Accessories**
Affect initiative, cooldowns, rerolls, or ability triggers.

Examples:

- Swift Band (+2 initiative)  
- Focus Lens (reroll 1 failed ability per battle)  
- Energy Coil (reduce cooldowns by 1)  

### **1.4 Consumable Accessories**
Single‑use items with strong effects.

Examples:

- Healing Herb  
- Burst Crystal  
- Elemental Bomb  

--------------------

## **2. Accessory Slots**

### **Player Slots**
- 2 base slots  
- +1 at higher levels  
- +1 from guild rank bonuses  

### **Sprite Slots**
- 1 base slot  
- +1 from evolution  
- +1 from rare traits  

--------------------

## **3. Accessory Rarity**

| Rarity | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Common** | Small, reliable bonuses |
| **Uncommon** | Moderate bonuses, minor effects |
| **Rare** | Strong effects, conditional triggers |
| **Epic** | Multi‑effect items |
| **Legendary** | Unique, build‑defining items |

--------------------

## **4. Accessory Loss on Defeat**

If a player dies:

- They lose **some accessories**  
- Unless the opponent chooses mercy  
- Higher rarity items have lower drop chance  

This reinforces:

- Risk vs reward  
- Social dynamics  
- Guild honor systems  

--------------------

## **5. Accessory Synergy**

Accessories can synergize with:

- Elemental abilities  
- Dice modifiers  
- Status effects  
- Guild bonuses  
- Sprite evolution traits  

--------------------

## **6. Future Expansion Hooks**

- Set bonuses  
- Craftable accessories  
- Guild‑exclusive accessories  
- Raid‑tier accessories  
- Corrupted accessories with drawbacks
