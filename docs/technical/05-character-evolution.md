## Evolution Mechanics

### Level 1 → Level 2 Evolution

**Requirements to Evolve**

| Requirement | Amount |
|------------|--------|
| XP Earned | 5,000 XP |
| Main Quests | 10 completed |
| Sprite Evolution | 1 sprite to stage 2 |
| Guild Events | 5 participated |
| Battle Victories | 20 won |

**Evolution Process**

1. Player returns to **Tree of Life center**
2. Aether energy flows from the ancient roots
3. Character is lifted into air with golden light
4. Transformation animation plays (2-3 seconds)
5. New Guardian form revealed with fanfare
6. Character lands with new appearance
7. **New Guardian ability card is immediately minted on Solana**
8. Player receives evolution notification
9. Guild members are notified of evolution
10. +1000 XP bonus for completing evolution

**On-Chain Recording**
- Character evolution stored on Solana blockchain
- Ability card NFT metadata updated
- Evolution timestamp recorded
- Can be viewed in wallet

---

### Level 2 → Level 3 Evolution

**Requirements to Evolve**

| Requirement | Amount |
|------------|--------|
| XP Earned | 25,000 XP |
| Main Quests | 30 completed |
| Sprite Evolution | 1 sprite to stage 3 |
| Tournament Wins | 5 tournament matches |
| Raid Leadership | Lead 1 successful raid |
| Aether Point Access | Unlock Level 3 Aether Points |

**Evolution Process**

1. Player must visit **Council Hall** at Level 3 territory
2. Council member conducts evolution ceremony
3. Aether energy intensifies around character
4. All floating crystals/elements appear
5. Transformation animation plays (3-4 seconds)
6. Council form revealed in spectacular fashion
7. Entire guild receives notification
8. **Ultimate ability card is minted on Solana**
9. Player receives title: "[Guild Name] Council Member"
10. +5000 XP bonus for completing evolution
11. Permanent guild status upgrade

**Benefits of Level 3**
- Access to Aether Points (special zones)
- Tournament organization privileges
- Raid leadership abilities
- Guild policy voting rights
- Council-exclusive items in market
- Special cosmetic armor variations
- Legendary seed access

## Character Evolution Mechanics

### Differences Between Root and Roothi

**Root**: Focuses on defensive progression
- Starts with higher Defense
- HP scales higher
- Guardian and Council forms are heavily armored
- Better survival in long battles

**Roothi**: Focuses on offensive and speed progression
- Starts with higher Speed and Attack
- Speed scales higher per level
- Guardian and Council forms are elegant and mobile
- Better damage output and evasion

### Ability Evolution

Each level provides a new unique ability:

1. **Level 1 Ability** - Basic ability card (AI-generated from dice roll)
2. **Level 2 Guardian Ability** - Enhanced ability (AI-generated from level + guild + character type)
3. **Level 3 Council Ability** - Ultimate ability (AI-generated, most powerful)

Each ability is **minted as an NFT** and stored permanently on Solana blockchain.

### Sprite Evolution and Character Evolution

Character evolution and sprite evolution are intertwined:
- Must evolve at least 1 sprite to stage 2 to reach Level 2
- Must evolve at least 1 sprite to stage 3 to reach Level 3
- Encourages player investment in sprite care system

---

**Character evolution is a major milestone that changes not only appearance but also unlocks new abilities, increases stats significantly, and opens access to higher-level content and guild responsibilities.**
