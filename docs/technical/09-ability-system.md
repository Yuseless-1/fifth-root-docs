# **Ability System — Full Specification**

Abilities define the tactical identity of both sprites and players. They introduce elemental interactions, status effects, burst windows, defensive counters, and strategic decision‑making.

--------------------

## **1. Ability Categories**

### **1.1 Attack Abilities**
Deal direct damage.  
Examples: strikes, elemental blasts, multi‑hit attacks.

### **1.2 Defense Abilities**
Reduce incoming damage or block attacks.  
Examples: shields, barriers, evasive moves.

### **1.3 Elemental Abilities**
Interact with guild elements.  
Examples: burn, soak, root, overload.

### **1.4 Status Abilities**
Apply buffs or debuffs.  
Examples: poison, regen, haste, weaken.

### **1.5 Ultimate Abilities**
High‑impact, limited‑use abilities.  
Triggered by D20 rolls or cooldown milestones.

--------------------

## **2. Ability Structure**

Each ability has:

- **Name**  
- **Type**  
- **Element** (optional)  
- **Activation Cost**  
- **Dice Requirement**  
- **Effect**  
- **Cooldown**  
- **Rank Requirement** (for guild abilities)  

--------------------

## **3. Ability Activation**

### **3.1 Standard Activation**
Roll a D8:

- 1–3 → Fail  
- 4–7 → Success  
- 8 → Enhanced success  

### **3.2 Cooldowns**
Cooldowns measured in turns:

- Minor abilities: 1–2 turns  
- Major abilities: 3–4 turns  
- Ultimates: 5+ turns or special trigger  

### **3.3 Conditional Abilities**
Some abilities require:

- Low HP  
- Elemental advantage  
- Status effect present  
- Sprite-only or player-only state  

--------------------

## **4. Elemental Interactions**

Each element has strengths and weaknesses:

- Fire beats Nature  
- Nature beats Energy  
- Energy beats Water  
- Water beats Fire  

Elemental abilities may:

- Increase damage  
- Apply special effects  
- Reduce cooldowns  
- Trigger bonus dice  

--------------------

## **5. Ultimate Abilities**

Ultimates are rare, powerful, and battle‑defining.

Triggered by:

- D20 roll  
- Charge meter  
- Rank 3 guild bonuses  
- Special conditions  

Ultimates often include:

- Multi‑dice rolls  
- Multi‑target effects  
- Guaranteed crits  
- Status resets  

--------------------

## **6. Ability Growth**

Sprites unlock new abilities through:

- Leveling  
- Evolution  
- Guild rank  
- Special quests  

Players unlock abilities through:

- Class progression  
- Guild rank  
- Accessories
