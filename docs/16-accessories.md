# Accessories & Items

## Equip Rules

Every player may equip **3 accessories** at a time, regardless of tier. Accessories can be swapped freely between battles but never during one.

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Slots | 3 for all tiers — Squire, Warrior, and Mage alike |
| Swapping | Free between battles; locked once a battle begins |
| Stacking | Two copies of the same accessory do **not** stack — the higher bonus applies only |
| Tier locks | Rare accessories require Warrior tier; Legendary requires Mage tier |
| Trading | Common/Uncommon trade freely. Rare+ are NFTs (see [11-cards-and-web3.md](11-cards-and-web3.md)) |
| Loss on raid | Accessories are **never** lost in a Raid — only $ROOT5, XP, and Sprites are at stake |

| ![Tidesear Bracelets](https://cdn.root5dao.com/sites/fifth-root-tcg/assets/accessories/tidesear-bracelets/tidesear-bracelets.webp) | **Tidesear Bracelets** | WIS +4; Water abilities deal +8% bonus damage; cleanse minor debuffs | Water Guild market / *The Abyssal Sanctum* expedition |
> Accessories are worn, not consumed. Healing items are consumed, not worn. They do not share slots.

## Accessory Set Bonuses

Beyond individual power, certain accessories are thematically linked and, when equipped together, grant **Set Bonuses**. A set bonus activates when a player wears 2 or 3 accessories from the same set simultaneously.

| Set | Pieces | 2-Piece Bonus | 3-Piece Bonus |
|-----|--------|---------------|---------------|
| **Root-Bound** | Root Band + Greenhouse Gloves | +5 HP; farm plots yield +2 $ROOT5 per harvest | +10 HP; +1 DEF; farm plots yield +4 $ROOT5 per harvest |
| **Guild Protector** | Rootweave Armor + Counter Wrap + Patrol Lantern | +1 Counter Threshold; +10% Raid defense | +2 Counter Threshold; +15% Raid defense; reveals incoming Raid 2 hours early |
| **Aether Adept** | Aether Stone Pendant + Mageweave Cloak | Aether abilities cost −5 additional SP | Aether abilities cost −10 additional SP; WIS +2 |
| **Guild Conqueror** | Any two Guild-specific Rare accessories (Rootweave Armor, Flameshod Boots, Tide Sigil, Storm Crown) | +2 to the secondary stat of each equipped piece; glowing aura during Gate Battles | *(3-piece not possible — only 4 guilds exist and each piece is single-guild)* |

Sets cannot be mixed across tiers for the same bonus — a Root Band (Common) combined with Greenhouse Gloves (Uncommon) still activates the Root-Bound set because the set is defined by identity, not rarity.
