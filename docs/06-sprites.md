# Sprites

## What Are Sprites?

Sprites are creature companions grown from Aether seeds in the Guild Greenhouse. They are not pets — they are partners. Each Sprite is unique, minted onto the blockchain at the moment of sprouting, and bound permanently to the player's web3 wallet.

Sprites accompany players into battle, contribute passive abilities, and can be leveled up from Level 1 to Level 2. At Level 2, they transform into their evolved form.

---

## Growing a Sprite

### Step 1 — Obtain a Seed

![Sprite Seed]()

Seeds are found through:
- Completing quests (all tiers drop seeds)
- Purchasing from the Guild Market
- Trading with other players
- Rare drop from Gate Battle victories

Every Sprite Seed looks identical — a dark teal, egg-shaped mass of bark and wood, with two hollow black eyes that stare without seeing, and a small crown of leaves already pushing out from the top. Bright green Aether energy crackles around it like living flame. It is impossible to tell from the outside what is growing within. The Aether keeps its secret until the moment of Sprouting.

> *"It already has eyes. It is already watching. You are not choosing what to grow — you are being chosen."* — old Greenhouse saying

Seeds do not reveal which Sprite they contain. The contents are unknown until Sprouting.

### Step 2 — Plant the Seed

The seed is planted in the **Guild Greenhouse**. Once planted, an **Incubation Progress Bar** begins (starts at 0%).

### Step 3 — Daily Tending

The seed must be tended daily. Each day without tending causes the progress bar to decrease by **5%**.

| Tending Action | Progress Gained |
|----------------|----------------|
| Water the seed (player action) | +8% |
| Seedagon Sprite tends the seed | +6% (passive, no player action needed) |
| Double-tend (Energy Guild weekly boost) | +16% (once per week) |

### Step 4 — Sprouting

When the progress bar reaches **100%**, the Sprite is ready to sprout. The player rolls **1d12** to determine which Sprite species hatches:

| Roll | Sprite (Level 1) | Evolved (Level 2) | Guild Affinity |
|------|-----------------|-------------------|----------------|
| 1–2 | Seedagon | Rootagon | Ground |
| 3–4 | Arbitos | Timberward | Energy |
| 5–6 | Fernad | Fernguard | Water |
| 7–8 | Onyx | Obsidia | Fire |
| 9–10 | Seedagon | Rootagon | Ground (common pool repeat) |
| 11 | Fernad | Fernguard | Water (common pool repeat) |
| 12 | Sap | Sap | None (All Guilds) |

> **Roll 12 — Sap:** Extraordinarily rare (< 1 in 200 hatches under normal conditions). Sap can only be rolled on a 12, and only if the player tended the seed every single day of incubation without a single missed day. If any day was missed, Sap cannot hatch regardless of the roll.

### Step 5 — Minting

Upon sprouting, the player chooses a blockchain network and the Sprite is **minted as an NFT** to their linked wallet. The Sprite's:
- Species
- Guild affinity
- Stats
- Hatch date
- Unique identifier

...are all written to the chain. The physical card equivalent can then be ordered through the game's card printing service (see [11-cards-and-web3.md](11-cards-and-web3.md)).

### Seedagon → Rootagon
**Guild Affinity:** Ground  
**Role:** Tender / Support

![Seedagon — Level 1](https://cdn.root5dao.com/sites/fifth-root-tcg/assets/sprites/seedagon/seedagon.webp)

Seedagon is the friendliest thing in the ROOT Kingdom and it knows it. A small, round creature with a soft fuzzy green head and a wide crown of large flat leaves fanning outward like a sunburst halo. Its eyes are two simple black dots, its smile is small and genuine. It wears an oversized bark-brown coat several sizes too big, the fabric threaded through with bright green Aether root veins that branch and spread like a living map. In one hand it holds a wooden shovel — its most important possession — planted in a small mound of fresh earth. It is already working before you ask it to. It does not look like a fighter. It is not a fighter. It is the Sprite that makes everything else possible — the one that tends the seeds, keeps the greenhouse, and shows up quietly when something needs caring for. Rootagon is simply what Seedagon looks like after years of doing exactly this, every single day.

## Sprite in Battle

Each Sprite a player carries can be deployed in battle once per match:

- Deploying a Sprite costs **0 SP** but uses your turn.
- The Sprite takes its own action on the following turn.
- Sprites can be knocked out (HP → 0) and cannot be redeployed in that battle.
- Knocked-out Sprites recover fully after battle (no permanent damage).

Players at **Squire** tier can deploy 1 Sprite per battle.  
**Warriors** can deploy 2.  
**Mages** can deploy 3.

## Sprite Leveling (Level 1 → Level 2)

Sprites level up based on their evolution condition (see individual profiles above). Once the condition is met:

1. The player initiates the **Sprite Evolution Ritual** at the Guild Greenhouse.
2. The old NFT is **burned** (invalidated on-chain).
3. A new Level 2 NFT is **minted** to the player's wallet with the evolved stats.
4. The physical card, if printed, can be exchanged for a Level 2 card at designated printing events.
## Sprite Traits

While every Sprite belongs to a species and guild affinity, individual Sprites also carry **Traits** — minor variations in temperament and Aether resonance that subtly influence their behavior and capabilities. Traits are not chosen; they emerge during incubation based on the specific conditions the seed experienced.

### How Traits Form

During the incubation period (see [Growing a Sprite](#growing-a-sprite)), a seed absorbs Aether from its surroundings. The exact composition of that Aether — shaped by the local Guild Territory, the tending actions performed, and small random fluctuations — determines the Sprite's trait:

- **Stubborn** — The seed was tended unevenly (missed days followed by double-tending). Sprite gains +1 DEF but −1 SPD.
- **Quickened** — The seed was tended consistently with no missed days. Sprite gains +1 SPD but −1 DEF.
- **Deep-rooted** — The seed was planted near a Root-ring or Aether pool. Sprite gains +10 max HP at Level 1.
- **Storm-touched** — The seed was incubated during a Storm-hum event (near Energy territory). Sprite gains +2 to turn order rolls when the owner is Energy Guild.
- **Windwhisper** — The seed was tended while near a Wind Guild territory or during a Gale-hum event. Sprite gains +2 to all rolls when owner is in Wind Guild, but −1 DEF.
- **Deep Echo** — The seed absorbed Aether during a Resonant Hum event from an ancient Root-ring. Sprite gains +1 to all rolls when owner is in Water or Earth Guild, and inflicts an additional 2 HP of Echo Damage on critical hits.

Traits are cosmetic in flavor but carry small mechanical effects. They do not change the Sprite's species, guild affinity, or evolution path — they simply add a layer of individuality.

### Checking a Sprite's Trait

A Sprite's trait is visible in its Profile section (above) and can be confirmed at any time by speaking to Chad in the village center. Chad's Aether hum reveals the trait name and its effect. Players cannot change a Sprite's trait after sprouting.

### Trait and Battle

Traits do not change a Sprite's Active or Passive abilities. Their mechanical effects are passive and always active. In the Sprite in Battle section, a Sprite with a trait gains its trait bonus in addition to its species stats and abilities.

> *"No two Seedagons are alike — and that is the point. The Tree does not make copies."* — Greenhouse saying
## Sprite Kinship

Beyond traits and stats, every Sprite develops a **Kinship** score with its owner — a measure of the trust and Aether resonance built through daily care, battle, and shared experience. Kinship begins at **0** at sprouting and can rise to a maximum of **100**.

### Earning Kinship

Kinship increases through consistent, positive interactions:

| Action | Kinship Gained |
|--------|---------------|
| Daily tending (on time, no misses) | +3 |
| Training together (Skill Transfer) | +5 |
| Winning a battle alongside your Sprite | +4 |
| Missing a day of tending | −5 |
| Sprouting a second Sprite (split attention) | −10 |

Kinship gains are capped at **+15 per in-game day** to prevent rapid stacking.

### Kinship Tiers

As Kinship grows, the Sprite's bond with its owner deepens, unlocking passive benefits:

| Kinship Tier | Range | Bonus |
|-------------|-------|-------|
| **Stranger** | 0–19 | No bonus |
| **Acquaintance** | 20–39 | +1% turn order |
| **Partner** | 40–59 | +2% turn order; +10 max HP |
| **Companion** | 60–79 | +3% turn order; +20 max HP; +1 to all rolls when owner is in the same guild as the Sprite's affinity |
| **Kindred** | 80–100 | +5% turn order; +30 max HP; all abilities gain +1 to cooldown reduction |

Kinship tiers are visible in the Sprite's Profile and are confirmed by speaking to Chad in the village center — Chad's Aether hum intensifies at each tier, resonating at a higher pitch as the bond deepens.

### Kinship Decay

If a player goes more than **7 consecutive days** without tending their Sprite, Kinship begins to decay at a rate of **−5 per day** until tending resumes. If Kinship drops to 0, the Sprite becomes **Unbound** — its stats revert to base values and its trait bonus is suspended until Kinship reaches 20 again. An Unbound Sprite cannot participate in Gate Battles or Raids until re-bonded.

> *"The strongest root is not the deepest — it is the one that never stopped reaching."* — Greenhouse saying

### Additional Traits

As the Tree's Aether flows through the ROOT Kingdom, Sprites occasionally manifest traits beyond the four standard varieties. These rarer traits emerge under more unusual incubation conditions:

- **Thornweave** — The seed was tended near a Ground Guild border where the soil is rich with protective thorns. The Sprite developed a hardy exterior. +1 DEF, −1 ATK.
- **Moonlit** — The seed was incubated during a rare lunar convergence event, when the Aether currents slow and cool. +1 SPD, −1 DEF.
- **Blightmarked** — The seed was briefly exposed to a corrupted Aether seep — a dark stain in an otherwise pure current. The Sprite carries a mark of shadow. +2 to all rolls against targets that are debuffed, but takes 1 extra damage per turn from all sources.
- **Sunforged** — The seed was incubated in a Greenhouse blessed by a Convergence Shrine ritual. The Sprite's Aether is exceptionally pure. All healing items the Sprite generates are +50% effective (rounded up).

> *The Tree has many faces. Four are named, but the roots run deeper than we know.* — Greenhouse elder
