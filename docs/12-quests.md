# Quests

## Overview

Quests are the primary way players explore the ROOT Kingdom, learn its lore, earn XP, and find rare items. Quests are tiered — a player can only access quests available at their current tier.

---

## Quest Tiers

| Tier | Available To | XP Range | Unlock |
|------|-------------|----------|--------|
| Side Quests | Squire, Warrior, Mage | 20–50 XP | From birth |
| Tier Quests | Warrior, Mage | 100–200 XP | Reach Warrior tier |
| Final Quests | Mage only | 300–500 XP | Reach Mage tier |
| Fifth Root Quest | Mage (special) | 1,000 XP | Complete all 3 Final Quests |

## Final Quests (Mage)

Final Quests are the most demanding content in the game — long quest chains requiring Mage-tier abilities, cross-guild cooperation, and combat against elite NPC opponents. There are **3 Final Quests**, and all must be completed to unlock the Fifth Root Quest.

### Final Quest 1 — The Deep Root

> *"Below the village, below the greenhouse, below the oldest stone — a root runs. Follow it."*

**Objective:** Explore the underground Root Network beneath your Guild Village. Solve three environmental puzzles using your guild abilities, and defeat the **Ancient Seedling** — a powerful NPC Sprite boss that has gone feral without tending.

**Boss: Ancient Seedling**
- HP: 500
- Abilities: Thorn Barrage (30 dmg, 3-hit), Overgrowth (Rooted + Weakened on target), Regen (heals 20 HP/turn)
- Weakness: Fire abilities deal ×1.5 damage; Water abilities do ×0.7

**Reward:** 350 XP, Aether Stone Pendant (Accessory), unlock of Mage-exclusive area in the map

---

### Final Quest 2 — The Counsel of Ancients

> *"The Tree remembers. Speak to those who were there at the beginning."*

**Objective:** Locate 4 Ancient Spirits (one in each guild territory) and answer their questions correctly (lore-based dialogue choices). Each Ancient Spirit challenges the player to a brief combat test before speaking.

**Ancient Spirit Bosses:**
- Each has 300 HP and uses abilities from their respective guild at Mage tier.
- No turns or dice — these fights play out as structured volleys (each boss uses 3 abilities in a fixed sequence; player must counter optimally).

**Reward:** 400 XP, Mageweave Cloak (Accessory), Ability Scroll (1 Mage ability of player's choice from their guild list)

---

### Final Quest 3 — Guard the Flag

> *"The flag has fallen once. It will not fall again — not on your watch."*

**Objective:** Lead a defense of your Guild Village in a **simulated Raid scenario** — a 3-wave NPC raid where the player acts as Champion Defender in all three zones.

**Waves:**
- Wave 1: 2 NPC Warriors (each 250 HP)
- Wave 2: 3 NPC Warriors (each 280 HP) — player can deploy 2 Sprites
- Wave 3: 1 NPC Mage (450 HP) — final boss; uses full Mage ability kit from an opposing guild

**Reward:** 450 XP, Guild Points Card (Legendary), Flag Guardian Title (cosmetic badge on Character Card)

---

## The Fifth Root Quest

> *"The fifth root runs deeper than the others. What lives at its end has waited for someone worthy."*

**Eligibility:** Mage tier, all 3 Final Quests completed.

**This quest is solo only.** No Sprites, no allies.

**Objective:** Follow the Fifth Root through 5 sequential chambers, each testing a different aspect of the player:

| Chamber | Test | Mechanic |
|---------|------|---------|
| 1 — The Root of Strength | Defeat an echo of yourself | NPC with your exact current stats |
| 2 — The Root of Knowledge | Solve 5 lore riddles about ROOT Kingdom history | Dialogue / multiple choice |
| 3 — The Root of Bond | Your Sprites appear as echoes — but you cannot deploy them; must win alone | Standard battle, no Sprites |
| 4 — The Root of Balance | Fight a combined NPC drawing from all four guild ability sets | Strategic counter required |
| 5 — The Root of Aether | No abilities, no items, no Sprites. HP: 100 vs HP: 100. Pure dice. | Pure 1d20 vs 1d20; 5 rounds; highest total wins |

**Reward:** 1,000 XP, **Fifth Root Token** (Legendary Accessory — all stats +3, Aether abilities unlocked 1 tier early for future characters), **Fifth Root Title**, and a unique **Legendary Character Card** marking completion.

> *Only those who complete the Fifth Root Quest may mentor others attempting it.*
## Weekly Challenges

In addition to the tiered quests available at each level, the ROOT Kingdom offers **Weekly Challenges** — recurring objectives that refresh every in-game Monday. They are designed for all tiers and provide a steady source of XP, $ROOT5, and rare materials outside the main quest lines.

### How Weekly Challenges Work

Each week, the Guild Counsel announces **3 Weekly Challenges** at the beginning of the in-game week. Players may attempt any number of challenges, but only the first completion of each challenge per week earns the reward.

| Challenge Tier | Available To | Typical Reward |
|----------------|-------------|----------------|
| **Starter** | Squire and above | 30 XP + 15 $ROOT5 |
| **Standard** | Warrior and above | 60 XP + 30 $ROOT5 + 1 random Common Accessory |
| **Advanced** | Mage and above | 120 XP + 75 $ROOT5 + 1 random Uncommon Accessory |

Weekly Challenges rotate every in-game Monday (the day after a Peaceful Period ends or after a Raid cooldown expires). The Guild Counsel may announce special bonus challenges during Peaceful Periods or in the aftermath of a successful Raid, offering double rewards for community-building objectives.

> *"The Tree grows one ring at a time. The Kingdom grows one week at a time."* — Counsel saying## Cooperative Expeditions

Some quests are too large for a single player. **Cooperative Expeditions** are designed for groups of 2–3 players, scaling difficulty and rewards by party size. They offer an alternative path to Rare accessories, Ability Scrolls, and larger $ROOT5 payouts — for those willing to share the glory.

### Expedition Rules

- **Party Size:** 2–3 players. All members must be at the minimum tier listed for the expedition.
- **Formation:** Parties register at the Guild Village's Expedition Board. Once registered, the expedition is locked to that party — no late additions.
- **Scaling:** Enemy HP and number of waves scale with party size (×1.5 for duo, ×2.0 for trio).
- **Reward Splitting:** XP and $ROOT5 are split equally among party members. Items and Ability Scrolls are distributed by party vote.
- **Failure:** If all party members are knocked out, the expedition ends. No rewards are granted, but no penalties apply beyond standard Knockdown XP.

| **The Smoldering Ward** | Warrior+ | 2–3 | Fire Territory forge ruins | Defend a Fire Guild forge team repairing an abandoned battlefield crucible; hold off 4 waves of Aether-wild Sprites |
| **The Frozen Echo** | Warrior+ | 2–3 | Water Territory Glacial Shelf | Recover three Aether crystals from a crevasse while managing shifting ice terrain and roaming Ice Wisp NPCs |
| **The Conductive Path** | Warrior+ | 2–3 | Energy Territory storm field | Activate a chain of five relay crystals across a lightning-scarred ridge before a storm surge engulfs the route |

### Expedition Details

#### The Rootweb Tunnels

> *"The roots connect us all. Sometimes what crawls along them is not a friend."*

**Objectives:**
1. Navigate 3 tunnel chambers (environment puzzles require Ground or Water abilities to collapse root-blockages).
2. Defeat the **Rootweb Matron** (HP: 400 in duo, 550 in trio — AoE poison spray, summons Rootworm adds).
3. Seal the tunnel breach with a combined ability ritual (both party members must use an Earth ability in sequence).

**Rewards:** 200 XP split, 80 $ROOT5 split, **Rootweb Silk** (crafting material — used in Guild Forging), 25% chance of Uncommon Accessory drop per member.

#### The Smoldering Ward

> *"The forge never truly dies. It waits."*

**Objectives:**
1. Escort the forge team to the crucible (1 NPC, must survive).
2. Hold position through 4 waves of Sprites (scaling by party size).
3. The forge team completes repairs; party must survive until the signal flare fires.

**Rewards:** 220 XP split, 100 $ROOT5 split, **Ember-forged Token** (redeemable for one free Guild Forging use per character, one-time), 30% chance of Fire-affinity Ability Scroll.

#### The Frozen Echo

> *"The ice remembers what the water forgets."*

**Objectives:**
1. Descend three ice shelf levels; each level has a puzzle (arrange Aether crystals by colour pattern).
2. Avoid the **Ice Wisp** patrols (Wisp HP: 120; uses Frost Armor and Glacial Wave at reduced power; party may sneak past or fight).
3. Extract the three crystals and surface before a cave-in.

**Rewards:** 210 XP split, 90 $ROOT5 split, 1 **Permafrost Shard** per member (crafting material), 20% chance of Tidewater Vial ×2 per member.

#### The Conductive Path

> *"Lightning chooses the fastest path. Make sure that path is yours."*

**Objectives:**
1. Activate 5 relay crystals in order within 8 total turns (each activation costs 10 SP per party member).
2. A storm surge occurs after activation 3 — all party members must Defend or take 15 shock damage.
3. Reach the final relay and channel the surge into a safe discharge.

**Rewards:** 230 XP split, 110 $ROOT5 split, **Storm-scavenged Coil** (crafting material), 1 random Energy Ability Scroll (Warrior tier or below) per party.

> *"Solo strength wins duels. Shared strength wins kingdoms."* — Expedition Board inscription
#### Community Harvest Festival

> *The roots give, the roots receive. Balance in giving sustains the whole.* — Ground Guild proverb

**Objectives:**
1. Collect the following seasonal harvest items across the territory:
   - 20 Rootbark pieces (Ground ability harvest)
   - 15 Emberfruit (Fire territory)

#### Guild Festival Logistics

All quests during the **Harvest Festival** (Season of Rootbloom) follow the **Community Quest** designation:

- **No combat required** — quests focus on gathering, crafting, and cooperation rather than combat encounters.
- **Tier restriction:** Available to all tiers, but rewards scale with party tier (higher tiers receive better crafting materials).
- **Guild collaboration:** Players may cooperate across guilds during Community Quests, with each guild contributing their unique harvest abilities to the shared goal.

> *When guilds unite, even the hardest roots can be uprooted together.* — Harvest Steward saying

### Quest Assignment & Generation

Quests are not randomly assigned — they are curated by the Guild Counsel and the Expedition Board to ensure each player encounters content appropriate to their tier and guild. The assignment system follows three principles:

**Tier Matching:** A player is only offered quests at or below their current tier. Side Quests are available from birth; Tier Quests unlock at Warrior; Final Quests unlock at Mage. The Fifth Root Quest requires all three Final Quests completed.

**Guild Alignment:** Quest objectives often draw on the player's guild abilities and territory. A Ground Guild player receives quests involving earth, roots, and stone; a Fire Guild player receives fire-themed objectives. Cross-guild quests exist but are rarer and typically involve cooperation between neighboring guilds.

**Dynamic Scaling:** Enemy HP and difficulty in Tier Quests and Final Quests scale slightly based on the player's current stats — ensuring a fair challenge without being trivially easy or impossibly hard. This scaling is transparent and visible in the quest description before acceptance.
