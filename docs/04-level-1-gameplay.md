# Fifth Root: Level 1 Gameplay

The Sims-style RPG Experience for all players.

## Level 1 Territory Overview

Level 1 is the primary gameplay area where players of all three levels (Young, Guardian, and Council) interact. This is a Sims-style isometric RPG environment with multiple zones and activities.

Level 1 serves as the "starter zone" in complexity but remains relevant and rewarding for all player levels due to the multiplayer social and economic systems.

## Key Zones in Level 1

- **Central Plaza** - Social hub and spawn point
- **Training Grounds** - Practice battles and gain XP
- **Plant Garden** - Seed planting and sprite care
- **Guild Market** - Trading and item purchases
- **Guild Gate** - Territory entrance to defend
- **Council Hall** - Quest board and announcements

## Core Gameplay Activities

### 1. Training

Players join training sessions to gain experience and improve combat skills:

#### Practice Battles
- Fight training dummies controlled by AI
- Gain small XP rewards (20-50 XP per win)
- No risk of losing items or resources
- Learn battle mechanics in safe environment

#### Sparring Matches
- Fight other guild members (PvP)
- Gain moderate XP rewards (50-150 XP per win)
- Optional participation
- Builds competitive skill

#### Group Training Events
- Coordinated training with rewards
- Organized by Guardians or Council members
- XP multiplier bonus (1.5x XP)
- Guild reputation bonuses

#### Sprite Training
- Level up sprites alongside character
- Sprites gain XP from battles
- Sprites need wins to evolve
- Co-progression system

### 2. Sprite Care System

The Plant Garden is where players grow and nurture sprites. This Tamagotchi-style system requires regular care:

#### Planting a Seed

1. Choose a seed from inventory
2. Select empty garden plot
3. Seed is planted with planting animation
4. Incubation timer begins (48 hours real-time)

#### Care Actions

Three daily care actions are available:

**Water the Seed** 🌊
- Increases care score by 5 points
- Can be done 3 times per day
- Blue droplet button animation

**Feed Aether to Seed** ⚡
- Increases care score by 8 points
- Can be done 2 times per day
- Golden energy particle effect

**Check Health Status** ❤️
- Increases care score by 3 points
- No daily limit
- Shows current care status

#### Care Score System

The hidden care score ranges from 0-100:
- Each care action increases score
- Score determines hatch type:
  - **≥ 70**: Arbvorbis (defensive tree sprite)
  - **< 70**: Seedagon (offensive dragon sprite)

#### Incubation Timeline

```
Day 1: Seed planted | Care score building
Day 2: Visible growth | Root development
Day 2 (end): Ready to hatch | Care score finalized
Hatch: Sprite emerges with determined type
```

#### Hatch Event

When timer reaches zero:
1. Glowing light effect around seed
2. Seed shell cracks open
3. Sprite emerges with hatch animation
4. Type determined by care score
5. Sprite NFT minted on Solana
6. Sprite added to player's active roster

## Gate Defense System

Each guild has a gate that serves as the entrance to their territory. Players must defend this gate from raids by other guilds.

### Gate Mechanics

- **Gate Health**: Resets daily at midnight UTC
- **Defense Volunteers**: Players can sign up as defenders
- **Raid Notifications**: Defenders receive alerts when raids begin
- **Simultaneous Defense**: Multiple players can defend at once
- **Defense XP**: 200 XP per successful defense

### Defending the Gate

When a raid begins:
1. Defenders are notified in real-time
2. Defenders spawn at gate entrance
3. Attackers must battle through defenders
4. Each attacker-defender battle is 1v1
5. Multiple rounds possible (attackers vs available defenders)
6. Gate health decreases with each attacker victory
7. Defenders gain XP for each successful defense

## Raid System

Guilds can organize raids on other territories:

### Raid Types

**Capture the Flag**
- Reach enemy gate and capture flag
- Successful raids steal all market loot
- Defenders can repel attackers
- Raid duration: 1 hour

### Raid Initiation Process

1. Guardian or Council member proposes raid
2. Guild members vote (24-hour voting period)
3. Requires 60% approval to proceed
4. Organizer sets raid time (must be 48+ hours ahead)
5. Target guild is notified 24 hours before raid
6. Raid window: Player-chosen 1-hour window

### Raid Outcomes

#### If Attackers Win
- All market inventory transferred to winning guild
- Attackers gain 500 XP each
- Defending guild can negotiate buyback
- Defending guild can launch counter-raid
- Market inventory resets for defending guild

#### If Defenders Win
- Defenders gain 300 XP each
- Gate gets reinforced (extra defense for 7 days)
- Guild reputation boost (+50 points)
- Market looted goods returned

## Experience and Progression

### Experience Points (XP)

Players gain XP through various activities:

| Activity | XP Reward |
|----------|-----------|
| Winning practice battle | 20-50 XP |
| Winning sparring match | 50-150 XP |
| Completing quests | 100-500 XP |
| Training sessions | 20-50 XP |
| Successful gate defense | 200 XP |
| Participating in events | 100-1000 XP |
| Raid participation (win) | 500 XP |
| Raid participation (loss) | 100 XP |

### Leveling Requirements

To advance from Level 1 (Young) to Level 2 (Guardian):

**XP Requirement**
- Reach **5,000 XP**

**Quest Requirement**
- Complete at least **10 main quests**

**Sprite Requirement**
- Hatch and evolve **one sprite to stage 2**

**Guild Requirement**
- Participate in **5 guild training events**

**Combat Requirement**
- Win **20 battles** (practice or sparring)

Once all requirements are met, player can visit the **Council Hall** to trigger evolution.

## Quests System

### Quest Types

#### Main Quests
- Story-driven progression quests
- Required for level advancement
- Higher XP rewards (200-500 XP)
- Sequential (some unlock after others)
- Examples:
  - "Plant Your First Seed"
  - "Win Your First Battle"
  - "Hatch Your First Sprite"
  - "Participate in a Guild Event"

#### Daily Quests
- Reset at midnight UTC each day
- Small rewards (50-100 XP, 25 Aether)
- Optional participation
- Encourage daily logins
- Examples:
  - "Care for Your Sprite (3 times)"
  - "Win a Practice Battle"
  - "Visit the Market"

#### Guild Quests
- Cooperative, benefit entire guild
- Global guild objective
- Guild-wide reward multipliers
- Examples:
  - "Guild Members: Win 50 Battles This Week"
  - "Defend the Gate 10 Times"
  - "Hatch 20 Sprites (collective)"

#### Special Events
- Limited time quests
- Unique rewards
- Seasonal themes
- Examples:
  - Spring Blossom Festival
  - Summer XP Boost Event
  - Autumn Boss Raid Challenge

### Quest Interface

- Quest board in Council Hall
- Displays available and completed quests
- Progress tracking for multi-step quests
- Reward preview before completion
- Claim button to receive rewards
