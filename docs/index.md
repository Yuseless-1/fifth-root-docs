# The Fifth Root — Game Documentation

> *"From the roots of all things, life branches outward."*

**The Fifth Root** is a web3 hybrid Role-Playing Trading Card Game set in the ROOT Kingdom — a living world grown from the eternal Tree of Life. Players are born into one of four elemental Guilds, evolve through three character tiers, grow and battle with Sprite companions, and compete in guild warfare to protect their flag and earn glory.

## Quick-Start Summary

1. **Be Born** — choose Lady or Lord, get assigned a Guild and starting ability via dice roll.
2. **Tend Your Seed** — grow your first Sprite in the Guild Greenhouse.
3. **Train** — visit the Training Arena to learn skills and earn XP.
4. **Battle** — challenge other guilds at the North/South Gates.
5. **Evolve** — rise from Squire to Warrior to Mage and unlock the Aether.
6. **Govern** — reach Mage tier to sit on the Guild Counsel and shape the Kingdom.
